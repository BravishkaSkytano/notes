> **Overview:** depicted ordinary people and everyday life truthfully, without idealization.
> **Period:** [[19th Century]]
> **Region:** [[France]]  
> **Key Artists:** [[Gustave Courbet]], [[Jean-François Millet]], [[Édouard Manet]]

^tldr

## Characteristics

- Rural workers  
- Harsh realities  
- Social commentary  
- Earthy color palette
