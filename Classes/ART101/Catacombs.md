> [!NOTE]
> Underground burial chambers used by early [[Christianity|Christians]] (and sometimes Jews) from the 2nd to 5th centuries CE. They were dug beneath cities like Rome and included tunnels, tombs, and small chapels decorated with [[Classes/ART101/Fresco|frescoes]] showing biblical scenes and symbols of faith.

**Example:** The Catacombs of Priscilla in Rome, known for early [[Christianity|Christian]] wall paintings like “The Good Shepherd.”
