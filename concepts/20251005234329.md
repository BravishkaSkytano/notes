> [!summary] Archaic
> 
> *Adjective.*
> 
> 1. Something that is, or shares the characteristic of a much earlier, and often more primitive, period (approx. 600-480 BC). Namely, a period that develops into a [[20251005234700|Classical]] stage of civilization.
> 2. No longer current; antiquated, old, outdated, etc.
> 3. Characteristic of words and language that is no longer in regular use and is now considered rare or outdated.
