> [!tldr] Vault
> An **arched ceiling or roof** made of stone or brick, used to cover a space between walls or columns. Vaults help support the building’s weight and create a sense of height and grandeur in architecture. Different types include **barrel vaults**, **groin vaults**, and **ribbed vaults**.

^9b51d1

**Example:** The **ribbed vaults** of **[[Notre-Dame de Paris]]** in **Paris**.
